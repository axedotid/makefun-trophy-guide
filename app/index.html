Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. Free roam continues after the credits and everything left is still doable.

  1. Story — finish the story; do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia (only possible missable). Save at the start and end of every chapter. Do side quests as they appear.
  2. 100% checklist — after the credits, work Progress → Stats → 100% Completion: 200/277 vehicles, 100/255 animals, 300/397 stunts, 15/18 meditations, 70/80 challenges. Most side quests aren't required.
  3. Cleanup — Strangers & Freaks strands, robberies, garages, shops, leftover activities. Then Vice Got a Price! (platinum) pops on its own.

Difficulty ~4/10 · ~200 h · no missable trophies besides the possible Chapter 1 meditation · cheats disable trophies.
